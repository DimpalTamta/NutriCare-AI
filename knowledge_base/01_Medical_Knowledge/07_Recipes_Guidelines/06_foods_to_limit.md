# Foods to Limit During Cancer Treatment

## Introduction
*   Maintaining a healthy diet is a key part of your journey through cancer treatment.
*   Proper nutrition helps your body heal tissues and keep up the energy needed for recovery.
*   While eating a variety of foods is important, some choices may interfere with your health or worsen side effects.
*   An oncology registered dietitian can help you tailor these limits to your specific needs.

## Purpose
*   The main goal is to help you maintain a healthy weight and avoid malnutrition.
*   Limiting certain foods can reduce the severity of treatment side effects like nausea and mouth sores.
*   Following these guidelines helps protect your body from infections when your immune system is weak.
*   Healthy choices during treatment also support long-term cancer prevention and survivorship.

## Highly Processed Foods
*   Processed foods often lack the essential nutrients your body needs to rebuild tissues.
*   An "unhealthy diet" is a known risk factor for various types of cancer.
*   These foods may contribute to an unhealthy body mass index (BMI), which increases cancer risk.
*   Try to choose whole, nutrient-dense foods whenever possible to support your recovery.

## High Sugar Foods
*   Foods high in added sugar can cause unplanned weight gain, which may impact your health.
*   A high body mass index is linked to nearly 25% of all cancer deaths.
*   Excessive sugar provides "empty calories" that don't help your body heal as effectively as protein-rich foods.
*   Limiting sugary snacks leaves more room for the high-protein meals your body requires.

## High Sodium Foods
*   Excess salt can contribute to bloating and fluid retention during treatment.
*   High-sodium diets are generally linked to other noncommunicable diseases that can complicate your care.
*   Processed and canned items are often high in salt; look for "low-sodium" or fresh alternatives.
*   Focus on flavoring food with herbs and mild spices instead of excessive salt [Turn 3 Content].

## Alcohol
*   Alcohol consumption is a major chemical carcinogen and a leading risk factor for cancer.
*   It is responsible for a significant portion of cancer-related deaths globally.
*   Alcohol can irritate a sore mouth or throat and may worsen treatment side effects like nausea.
*   Avoiding or reducing alcohol is a primary recommendation for both prevention and recovery.

## Raw or Undercooked Foods
*   Raw or undercooked proteins can carry bacteria that are dangerous if your immune system is weak.
*   Avoid raw fish (sushi), undercooked meats, and "runny" eggs during treatment.
*   Ensure all animal products are cooked to the proper internal temperature to kill harmful germs.
*   Food safety is a top priority to prevent infections while your body is healing.

## Very Spicy Foods
*   Spicy flavors can cause significant pain if you have mouth sores or a sore throat from radiation or chemo.
*   Hot peppers and heavy spices may trigger or worsen nausea and vomiting.
*   These foods can also lead to digestive issues like gas, bloating, and diarrhea.
*   Switch to mild seasonings if you find spicy foods are becoming uncomfortable to eat.

## Excess Saturated Fat
*   Greasy, fried, and heavy foods are often difficult for the body to digest during treatment.
*   High-fat foods are a common trigger for nausea, vomiting, and stomach discomfort.
*   They can also worsen symptoms of gas and bloating.
*   While you need calories, focus on healthy fats like oils rather than heavy fried items.

## Unsafe Foods During Low Immunity
*   Avoid unpasteurized milk, cheese, and fruit juices, as they may contain live bacteria.
*   Stay away from raw sprouts (like alfalfa or bean sprouts) which are difficult to clean thoroughly.
*   Avoid well water unless it has been tested and filtered to remove potential contaminants like arsenic.
*   Be cautious with "buffet" style dining where food may have been sitting out at unsafe temperatures.

## Situations Where Foods May Need Limiting
*   **Mouth Sores:** Limit acidic (citrus), salty, crunchy, and spicy foods.
*   **Nausea/Vomiting:** Limit greasy, fried, and very sweet foods.
*   **Diarrhea:** Limit high-fat foods and sometimes high-fiber raw vegetables if they cause irritation.
*   **Swallowing Problems:** Limit dry, crumbly, or sharp-textured foods like crackers or chips.

## Key Facts
*   Nearly 10 million people died from cancer globally in 2024.
*   Approximately 38% of cancer cases can be prevented by avoiding risk factors like an unhealthy diet.
*   Nutritional care is a basic human right for all patients undergoing treatment.
*   Tobacco and alcohol remain the most significant modifiable risk factors for cancer.
