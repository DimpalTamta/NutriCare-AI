# Ingredient Substitutions for Cancer Nutrition Chatbot

## Introduction
*   Oncology registered dietitians provide **comprehensive, customized nutrition care** to help you stay healthy before, during, and after cancer treatment.
*   The goal of these substitutions is to help you make **informed choices** that meet your individual needs.
*   Nutritional care is recognized as a **human right** for all patients.

## Why Ingredient Substitutions are Important
*   Cancer treatments like chemotherapy, radiation, and surgery can cause a **loss of appetite and energy**.
*   Side effects like **mouth sores, taste changes, and swallowing problems** often require you to change what you eat.
*   Proper substitutions help you maintain a **healthy weight** and get enough **protein and calories** to heal properly.

## Protein Substitutions
*   **If you cannot eat meat or poultry:** Substitute with beans, lentils, or eggs.
*   **For difficulty chewing or swallowing:** Replace tough cuts of meat with **ground meats, soft fish, Greek yogurt, or cottage cheese**.
*   **To increase protein density:** Add **high-protein ingredients** like beans or liquid nutritional supplements to soups and casseroles.

## Carbohydrate Substitutions
*   **For energy needs:** If you cannot tolerate whole grains, use **soft starches** like mashed potatoes, white rice, or cooked pasta.
*   **For high-calorie goals:** Replace plain grains with versions prepared with **milk or added healthy fats**.

## Healthy Fat Substitutions
*   **To boost calories:** Add healthy fats like **olive oil, avocado, or nut butters** to meals if you are experiencing unplanned weight loss.
*   **Information not in sources:** Substitute butter with liquid oils (like canola or olive oil) for heart health while maintaining calorie goals.

## Dairy Alternatives
*   **If lactose-intolerant:** Use **lactose-free milk** or plant-based milks (like soy milk) that are high in protein.
*   **For high-protein needs:** Use **Greek yogurt** instead of regular yogurt for nearly double the protein content.

## Gluten-Free Alternatives
*   **Information not in sources:** Replace wheat-based products with **quinoa, rice, corn, or potatoes** to provide necessary energy.
*   **Information not in sources:** Use gluten-free flours (almond or coconut) to keep protein and calorie levels high.

## Vegetarian Alternatives
*   **Substitute meat with:** A wide variety of **beans, lentils, and chickpeas**.
*   **Boost protein with:** **Eggs and dairy products** like cheese or yogurt.

## Vegan Alternatives
*   **Substitute all animal protein with:** **Legumes, beans, lentils, and soy products**.
*   **Information not in sources:** Use nutritional yeast or nuts/seeds to add flavor and calories without animal products.

## Low-Spice Alternatives
*   **For mouth sores or inflammation:** Substitute spicy or acidic ingredients (like hot peppers or vinegar) with **mild herbs, cream-based sauces, or gravies**.
*   **Information not in sources:** Avoid citrus juices if they sting; use mild broths or diluted apple juice instead.

## Soft Food Alternatives
*   **For dry mouth or swallowing issues:** Replace dry, crunchy foods with **moist, soft options**.
*   **Use moistening agents:** Substitute dry bites with foods covered in **gravy, sauces, or broths** to make swallowing easier.

## Hydration-Friendly Ingredients
*   **If you struggle to drink water:** Use **broths, smoothies, or liquid nutritional supplements** to get both fluids and nutrients.
*   **Ensure safety:** Always use **safe drinking water** and avoid sources that may contain contaminants like arsenic.

## Substitution Rules
*   **Maintain Density:** Substitutions should never lower the overall **calorie or protein** content of the meal for patients losing weight.
*   **Adjust for Texture:** Always prioritize the **texture** that the patient can safely swallow (e.g., pureed vs. soft).
*   **Avoid Risk Factors:** Avoid or significantly reduce **alcohol** in all substitutions.
*   **Focus on Variety:** Encourage a **wide variety** of nutrient-dense substitutions to improve deficiencies.

## Key Facts
*   Cancer was responsible for nearly **10 million deaths** globally in 2024.
*   Approximately **38% of cancers** can be prevented through healthy lifestyle choices, including a healthy diet.
*   Low fruit and vegetable intake is a major **modifiable risk factor** for cancer death.
*   The primary nutritional goal during treatment is to **maintain a healthy weight**.
*   Nutritional care is recognized as a **human right** to support patient dignity.
