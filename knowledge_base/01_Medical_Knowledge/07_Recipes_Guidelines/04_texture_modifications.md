# Texture Modifications for Cancer Patients

## Introduction
*   Good nutrition is vital to help your body heal and maintain energy during cancer treatment.
*   Cancer treatments like radiation and chemotherapy can make it difficult to eat regular meals.
*   Changing the texture of your food makes it easier to get the nutrients you need to stay strong.

## Why Texture Modification is Needed
*   Treatment side effects can include mouth sores, dry mouth, or pain when swallowing.
*   Some patients experience "swallowing problems" that make hard or crunchy foods unsafe.
*   Feeling very weak or having a low appetite may make chewing a full meal difficult.
*   Proper texture selection helps prevent malnutrition and unplanned weight loss.

## Regular Diet
*   This includes all types of food with no changes to their texture.
*   It is meant for patients who have no trouble chewing or swallowing.
*   **Examples:** Raw vegetables, whole fruits, nuts, and standard meats.

## Soft Diet
*   Includes foods that are naturally soft or cooked until they are very tender.
*   These foods require very little chewing and are easy to break apart with a fork.
*   **Examples:** Flaky fish, well-cooked pasta, soft-boiled eggs, and canned fruits (without skins).

## Pureed Diet
*   Foods are blended, mashed, or strained until they are completely smooth.
*   The texture should be similar to pudding or thick gravy with no lumps.
*   **Examples:** Smooth mashed potatoes, blended vegetable soups, and Greek yogurt.

## Liquid Diet
*   This consists of foods that are in liquid form at room temperature.
*   It is often used when a patient cannot tolerate any solid or pureed textures.
*   **Examples:** Fruit juices, broths, milkshakes, and high-protein nutritional supplement drinks.

## Easy-to-Swallow Foods
*   Moist foods are generally easier to swallow than dry foods.
*   Add gravies, sauces, or broths to moisten meats and grains.
*   Cool or room-temperature foods may be more comfortable than very hot foods.
*   High-calorie and high-protein snacks like puddings or custards help maintain weight.

## Foods to Avoid
*   **Crunchy or Sharp Foods:** Chips, crackers, or raw carrots can scratch a sore mouth.
*   **Acidic or Spicy Foods:** Citrus fruits and hot peppers can sting if you have mouth sores.
*   **Dry or Crumbly Foods:** Plain bread or dry cake may cause coughing or be hard to move in the mouth.

## Texture Selection Guidelines
*   Ask your doctor for a referral to a registered dietitian (RD) if you have trouble eating.
*   An RD can help you find the right texture level based on your specific side effects.
*   The main goal is to maintain a healthy weight and rebuild body tissues.

## Key Facts
*   Cancer caused nearly 10 million deaths globally in 2024.
*   Up to 38% of cancer cases can be prevented through healthy lifestyle choices, including diet.
*   Nutritional care is recognized as a human right for all patients.
