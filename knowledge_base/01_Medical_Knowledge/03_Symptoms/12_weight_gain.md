# Managing Weight Gain During Cancer Treatment

## Introduction
*   While many people expect to lose weight during cancer care, **unplanned weight gain** can also occur.
*   Maintaining a healthy weight is a primary goal to help your body heal and stay strong.
*   Working with an oncology registered dietitian can help you create a balanced plan to manage these changes.

## What Causes Weight Gain?
*   **Medications:** Certain drugs, like steroids, can increase your appetite or cause your body to hold onto extra fluid.
*   **Hormone Therapy:** Treatments that affect your hormones often lead to weight changes.
*   **Decreased Activity:** Feeling tired or weak may lead to moving less, which can contribute to weight gain.
*   **Emotional Eating:** Stress, anxiety, or changes in your routine may change how you eat.

## Nutrition Goals
*   **Maintain a healthy weight:** This helps reduce the risk of other health issues and supports recovery.
*   **Eat a balanced diet:** Focus on getting the right mix of nutrients to keep your body functioning well.
*   **Control portions:** Ensure you are eating enough for energy without taking in more than your body needs.

## Healthy Weight Management
*   **Focus on whole foods:** Choose foods that are high in nutrients but lower in calories.
*   **Stay consistent:** Try to eat regular meals to avoid overeating later in the day.
*   **Ask for help:** Request a **nutrition referral** from your oncologist to speak with a specialist.

## Balanced Diet
*   **Fruits and vegetables:** Aim to fill half your plate with colorful produce.
*   **Lean proteins:** Include chicken, fish, beans, or tofu to help rebuild tissues.
*   **High-fiber grains:** Choose options like brown rice or whole-wheat bread to help you feel full longer.
*   **Stay hydrated:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day.

## Physical Activity
*   **Help digestion:** Regular movement can improve your appetite and help your body process food.
*   **Boost energy:** Walking is a proven way to reduce treatment-related fatigue.
*   **Keep a healthy weight:** Regular exercise is a key strategy for weight maintenance and cancer prevention.

## Foods to Limit
*   **Sugary drinks:** Limit sodas, sweetened teas, and processed juices.
*   **Highly processed snacks:** Reduce intake of foods high in solid fats and added sugars.
*   **Alcohol:** Avoiding or reducing alcohol is recommended for better health during and after treatment.

## Monitoring Weight
*   Check your weight regularly to track any sudden or steady increases.
*   Keep a log of what you eat and how much you move to share with your healthcare team.

## When to Contact a Healthcare Provider
*   If you notice a **rapid weight gain** (several pounds in a few days), which could be fluid retention.
*   If your appetite feels "out of control" due to medications.
*   If weight gain is causing you physical discomfort or making it hard to move.

## Frequently Asked Questions

*   **Why am I gaining weight during treatment?**
    *   It is often due to treatments like hormone therapy, steroids that increase appetite, or a decrease in your usual physical activity.
*   **How can I manage weight gain?**
    *   Focus on a balanced diet rich in plants and maintain a regular, light exercise routine like walking.
*   **What foods should I avoid?**
    *   Limit high-calorie, low-nutrient foods like sugary snacks, processed meats, and alcohol.
*   **Is exercise safe?**
    *   Yes, and it is specifically recommended to help with digestion and side effects during cancer care.
*   **When should I see a doctor?**
    *   Contact your team if weight changes are sudden or if you need professional guidance to manage your diet.

## Key Facts
*   Cancer was responsible for nearly **10 million deaths** globally in 2024.
*   About **38% of cancers** are preventable through healthy lifestyle factors, including maintaining a healthy weight.
*   Nutrition is a **human right**, and getting the right dietary support is essential for all patients.
