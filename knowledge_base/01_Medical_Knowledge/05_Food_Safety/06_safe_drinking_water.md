# Safe Drinking Water for Cancer Patients

## Introduction
*   Staying hydrated is a critical part of your cancer care and recovery journey.
*   Proper fluid intake helps your body function correctly and maintain the energy needed to handle the challenges of treatment.
*   Oncology registered dietitians provide specialized support to help you make informed choices about your hydration.

## Importance of Safe Drinking Water
*   **Body Functioning:** Fluids are absolutely essential for every system in your body to work correctly.
*   **Managing Side Effects:** Safe water is necessary to replace fluids lost due to treatment-related side effects like vomiting and diarrhea.
*   **Avoiding Malnutrition:** Proper hydration helps you maintain a healthy weight, which is the primary goal during cancer treatment.
*   **Preventing Infections:** Immunocompromised patients (those with weakened immune systems from chemotherapy or radiation) must take extra precautions, as their bodies have a harder time fighting off waterborne germs.

## Recommended Water Sources
*   **Public Water Systems:** Generally, city or municipal water is treated and monitored for safety.
*   **Private Wells:** **Information not in my sources:** If your home uses a private well, it should be tested regularly.
*   **Testing for Carcinogens:** Arsenic is a known drinking water contaminant and chemical carcinogen that should be avoided.

## Boiled Water
*   **Information not in my sources:**
    *   Boiling water is the most effective way to kill harmful bacteria, viruses, and parasites.
    *   Bring water to a rolling boil for at least one full minute.
    *   Let the water cool completely before drinking or using it to brush your teeth.
    *   Store boiled water in a clean, covered container in the refrigerator.

## Filtered Water
*   **Information not in my sources:**
    *   If you use a water filter, ensure it is certified by the National Sanitation Foundation (NSF).
    *   Look for filters specifically designed to remove "cysts" or labeled as "Absolute 1-micron" filters if you are severely immunocompromised.
    *   Change filter cartridges exactly as the manufacturer recommends to prevent germ buildup.

## Packaged Drinking Water
*   **Information not in my sources:**
    *   Bottled water is generally safe if it has been treated by distillation or reverse osmosis.
    *   Check labels for words like "distilled," "purified," or "filtered by reverse osmosis."
    *   Ensure the seal on the bottle is intact before opening.

## Unsafe Water Sources
*   **Untreated Sources:** Avoid drinking directly from lakes, rivers, streams, or springs.
*   **Contaminated Wells:** Do not use well water that has not been tested for chemical carcinogens like arsenic.
*   **Information not in my sources:** Avoid "raw" or unpasteurized water, which has not been treated to remove pathogens.

## Hydration Tips
*   **Small Sips:** If you are feeling nauseated or have a poor appetite, try taking small, frequent sips of water throughout the day.
*   **Gentle Activity:** Regular exercise can help improve your digestion and make it easier to stay hydrated.
*   **Personalized Plan:** Ask your oncology team for a referral to a registered dietitian to create a hydration plan tailored to your specific side effects and treatment.

## Key Facts
*   Cancer accounted for nearly **10 million deaths** globally in 2024, or roughly one in six deaths.
*   Approximately **38% of cancers are preventable** through healthy lifestyle choices, including a healthy diet and avoiding environmental contaminants.
*   Nutritional care, including access to safe fluids, is recognized as a **human right** for all patients.
*   Maintaining a **healthy weight** is the most important nutritional goal during active cancer treatment.
*   Arsenic is a significant chemical carcinogen that can contaminate drinking water supplies.
