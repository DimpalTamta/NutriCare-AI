# Safe Food Storage for Cancer Patients

## Introduction
*   Safe food storage is a crucial part of your cancer care, helping you stay strong and avoid infections during recovery,.
*   Following these guidelines helps ensure the food you eat is both nutritious and safe for your weakened immune system.
*   **Oncology registered dietitians** can provide customized support to help you manage your nutrition needs safely,.

## Why Food Storage Matters
*   **Weakened Immunity:** Treatments like chemotherapy and radiation can lower your body's ability to fight off germs found in improperly stored food.
*   **Infection Risk:** Contaminants like **aflatoxin** (a food contaminant) can pose a serious risk if food is not handled and stored correctly.
*   **Preventing Malnutrition:** Proper storage keeps food fresh so you can maintain the energy and weight needed for treatment.

## Refrigeration Guidelines
*   **Information not in sources:** Keep your refrigerator at or below 40°F (4°C) to slow the growth of harmful bacteria.
*   **Separate Raw and Cooked:** Always store raw meat, poultry, and seafood on the bottom shelf to prevent their juices from dripping onto ready-to-eat foods.
*   **Refrigerate Promptly:** Put leftovers in the fridge within two hours of cooking (or one hour if the temperature is above 90°F) [Information not in sources].

## Freezing Guidelines
*   **Information not in sources:** Keep your freezer at 0°F (-18°C) or below to keep food safe indefinitely, though quality may decrease over time.
*   **Label Everything:** Use tape or a marker to write the date and the name of the food on the container [Information not in sources].
*   **Use Moisture-Proof Bags:** This prevents "freezer burn," which can change the texture and taste of your food [Information not in sources].

## Shelf Life of Common Foods
*   **Information not in sources:**
    *   **Cooked leftovers (meat, poultry, stews):** 3 to 4 days in the fridge.
    *   **Fresh poultry or ground meat:** 1 to 2 days in the fridge.
    *   **Fresh beef or pork:** 3 to 5 days in the fridge.
    *   **Eggs (in shell):** 3 to 5 weeks in the fridge.

## Proper Food Containers
*   **Information not in sources:**
    *   Use shallow containers for leftovers so they cool down quickly in the refrigerator.
    *   Ensure all containers have airtight lids to keep germs out and moisture in.
    *   Choose BPA-free plastic or glass containers for storing and reheating food.

## Checking Expiry Dates
*   **Information not in sources:**
    *   **"Use-By" date:** This is the last date the product is at peak quality. Discard food after this date.
    *   **"Sell-By" date:** This tells the store how long to display the product. You should buy the product before this date.
    *   **If in doubt, throw it out:** Never taste food that you think might be expired to see if it is still good.

## Safe Thawing Methods
*   **Information not in sources:**
    *   **In the Fridge:** This is the safest way to thaw food. Plan ahead, as it may take 24 hours or more.
    *   **Cold Water:** Place food in a leak-proof bag and submerge it in cold tap water, changing the water every 30 minutes.
    *   **Microwave:** Only use this method if you plan to cook the food immediately afterward.

## Signs of Spoiled Food
*   **Information not in sources:**
    *   **Odors:** A sour or "off" smell is a primary indicator of spoilage.
    *   **Texture:** Look for sliminess in meats or mushiness in vegetables.
    *   **Appearance:** Visible mold, even if it is only on one small part of the food, means the entire item should be discarded.

## Key Facts
*   Cancer was responsible for nearly **10 million deaths** globally in 2024,.
*   Approximately **38% of cancers** are currently preventable through healthy lifestyle choices, including a healthy diet.
*   Nutritional care is recognized as a **human right** for all cancer patients to support their dignity and health.
*   Maintaining a **healthy weight** is the primary goal for anyone undergoing cancer treatment.
*   Early detection and proper nutrition can lead to a **high chance of cure** for many common cancers,.
