# Safe Food Preparation for Cancer Patients

## Introduction
*   Safe food preparation is a critical part of your care before, during, and after cancer treatment.
*   Oncology registered dietitians provide customized, scientifically supported care to help you make informed choices about your food.
*   Proper attention to food safety can assist in an easier recovery and help you maintain your strength.

## Importance of Safe Food Preparation
*   Cancer treatments like chemotherapy, radiation, and surgery can weaken your immune system.
*   A weakened immune system puts you at an increased risk for infections from foodborne germs or contaminants like aflatoxin.
*   Following safe kitchen practices helps prevent **malnutrition** and ensures you stay strong enough to complete your treatment.

## Preparing Raw Foods Safely
*   **Clean:** Always wash your hands and surfaces often when handling raw foods.
*   **Information not in sources:** Rinse all fresh fruits and vegetables thoroughly under cold, running water before peeling or cutting.
*   Eating a wide variety of fruits and vegetables is important for health, but they must be prepared carefully to avoid germs.

## Cooking Foods Thoroughly
*   **Cook:** Heating food to high temperatures kills most harmful bacteria.
*   **Information not in sources:** Avoid "lightly cooked" or raw animal products, such as soft-boiled eggs or rare steaks.
*   **Information not in sources:** Ensure all egg yolks and whites are firm.

## Safe Internal Cooking Temperatures
*   **Information not in sources:** Use a food thermometer to ensure meats reach safe temperatures (e.g., 165°F for poultry, 160°F for ground meats, and 145°F for whole cuts of beef or pork).
*   **Information not in sources:** Never rely on the color of meat alone to determine if it is done.

## Using Clean Water
*   Water can sometimes contain contaminants like arsenic, which are risk factors for health.
*   Ensure the water you use for drinking, cooking, and washing produce comes from a safe, treated source.
*   If you have a private well, ask your healthcare team if you should have it tested during your treatment.

## Avoiding Cross Contamination
*   **Separate:** Keep raw meat, poultry, and seafood separate from ready-to-eat foods like bread and fresh produce.
*   **Information not in sources:** Use separate plates and utensils for raw and cooked items to prevent spreading germs.

## Safe Cutting Boards
*   **Information not in sources:** Use one cutting board for fresh produce and a different one for raw meat, poultry, and seafood.
*   **Information not in sources:** Replace cutting boards that have become deeply scarred or hard to clean.

## Safe Kitchen Practices
*   **Chill:** Refrigerate or freeze meat, poultry, eggs, seafood, and other perishables within 2 hours of cooking or purchasing.
*   **Information not in sources:** Never thaw food at room temperature; use the refrigerator, cold water, or a microwave instead.
*   **Information not in sources:** Keep your refrigerator temperature at or below 40°F (4°C).

## Key Facts
*   Cancer accounted for nearly **10 million deaths** globally in 2024, or roughly one in six deaths.
*   Approximately **38% of cancers** are currently preventable through healthy lifestyle choices, including a healthy diet.
*   Nutritional care is recognized as a **human right** for all patients.
*   Lung, breast, and colorectal cancers are the most common types diagnosed worldwide.
*   Many cancers have a high chance of being cured if detected early and treated according to best practices.
