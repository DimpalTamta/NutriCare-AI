# Food Hygiene for Cancer Patients

## Introduction
*   Safe food handling is a vital part of your cancer care and recovery.
*   Proper hygiene helps you stay nourished while protecting your body from unnecessary infections.
*   Working with an oncology registered dietitian can help you create a safe and healthy eating plan.

## Why Food Hygiene is Important for Cancer Patients
*   **Weakened Immune System:** Cancer treatments like chemotherapy, radiation, and surgery can lower your body's ability to fight germs.
*   **Infection Risk:** A weakened system makes it much easier to get sick from bacteria or viruses found in food.
*   **Treatment Success:** Preventing foodborne illness helps you avoid complications that could delay your scheduled treatments.

## How Germs Spread Through Food
*   **Contaminants:** Germs can enter food through contaminated water or external agents.
*   **Poor Handling:** Bacteria, viruses, and parasites can live on raw or improperly stored food.
*   **Toxins:** Certain contaminants, such as aflatoxin, can occur naturally in food and increase health risks.

## Personal Hygiene Before Eating
*   **Information not in sources:**
    *   Always change into clean clothes if you have been working in the garden or with pets.
    *   Keep your hair tied back and remove jewelry that might collect germs before cooking.
    *   Cover any cuts or sores on your hands with a clean bandage.

## Hand Washing Guidelines
*   **Information not in sources:**
    *   Wash your hands with warm water and soap for at least 20 seconds.
    *   Scrub your wrists, between fingers, and under your fingernails.
    *   Wash your hands before, during, and after preparing any food.
    *   Always wash your hands after using the bathroom, touching pets, or handling trash.

## Kitchen Hygiene
*   **Information not in sources:**
    *   Keep all countertops and cooking surfaces clean and sanitized.
    *   Wash dishcloths and sponges frequently in hot water.
    *   Clean your refrigerator regularly to prevent the growth of mold or bacteria.
    *   Keep your kitchen area free from insects and pests.

## Cleaning Fruits and Vegetables
*   **Essential Nutrients:** Eating a variety of fruits and vegetables is critical for reducing the cancer burden and helping you heal.
*   **Information not in sources:**
    *   Rinse all produce thoroughly under cold, running water before peeling or cutting.
    *   Use a clean brush to scrub firm produce like potatoes or melons.
    *   Avoid using soaps, detergents, or commercial "food washes."
    *   Discard the outer leaves of leafy greens like lettuce or cabbage.

## Preventing Cross Contamination
*   **Information not in sources:**
    *   Keep raw meat, poultry, and seafood separate from ready-to-eat foods in your grocery cart and refrigerator.
    *   Use one cutting board for fresh produce and a separate one for raw meat.
    *   Never place cooked food back on a plate that previously held raw meat.
    *   Wash all surfaces and utensils that have touched raw meat immediately.

## Safe Utensils and Equipment
*   **Information not in sources:**
    *   Wash all utensils, including knives and spoons, in hot, soapy water or a dishwasher.
    *   Pay special attention to equipment with moving parts, like can openers or blenders, which can hide germs.
    *   Replace worn-out cutting boards that have deep grooves or cracks.

## Key Facts
*   Approximately **38% of cancers are preventable** through healthy lifestyle factors, including a safe, healthy diet.
*   Cancer was responsible for nearly **10 million deaths** globally in 2024.
*   Nutritional care, including food safety support, is a **human right** for all patients.
*   The primary goal during treatment is to **maintain a healthy weight**.
*   Low fruit and vegetable intake is a major modifiable risk factor for many types of cancer.
