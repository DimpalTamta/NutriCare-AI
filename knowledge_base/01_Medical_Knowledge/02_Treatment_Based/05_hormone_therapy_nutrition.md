# Nutrition During Hormone Therapy

## Introduction
*   Nutrition is a vital part of the cancer journey, helping patients and survivors manage the physical changes that occur during treatment.
*   Eating right and staying active are key components in reducing risks and supporting overall health.
*   Maintaining a healthy nutritional status is recognized as a fundamental human right for every patient.

## What is Hormone Therapy?
*   Hormone therapy is a type of cancer treatment that addresses the role of hormones in the body.
*   For example, research indicates that certain hormones associated with development can influence the growth of specific tumors.
*   It is one of many treatment options used alongside chemotherapy, radiation, and surgery to manage cancer.

## Common Nutrition Challenges
*   **Cancer-Related Fatigue:** Feeling extremely tired is a common challenge that can impact your ability to eat and stay active.
*   **Weight Changes:** Maintaining a stable, healthy weight can be difficult during and after treatment.
*   **Microbiome Health:** Many people do not get enough fiber, which is necessary to keep the gut microbiome healthy during care.

## Weight Management
*   Making healthy food choices and staying active are the most effective ways to manage your weight.
*   Staying at a healthy weight is important for your overall recovery and long-term health.
*   Regularly monitoring your weight and discussing changes with your care team can help you stay on track.

## Bone Health
*   General healthy eating and staying active support the strength of your body during treatment.
*   Following recommended screening guidelines is a critical part of monitoring your health as a survivor.
*   Nutrition and physical activity work together to help preserve your physical function.

## Heart-Healthy Eating
*   Focus on a balanced approach to eating by including a variety of nutrient-dense foods.
*   Making healthy choices helps reduce the risk of long-term health complications.
*   A diet that supports your heart is essential for overall wellness throughout your survivorship.

## Recommended Foods
*   **Fiber-Rich Foods:** Incorporate plenty of beans, grains, and vegetables to feed your microbiome and keep your digestion regular.
*   **Variety:** Aim for a wide variety of foods to ensure your body gets the nutrients it needs to recover.
*   **Whole Foods:** Focus on "eating right" by choosing fresh and nutrient-dense options whenever possible.

## Foods to Limit
*   The sources recommend general healthy choices to reduce risk, which typically includes limiting foods that do not provide high nutritional value.
*   It is important to follow guidance on eating right to help your body stay as strong as possible.

## Exercise and Nutrition
*   **Walking:** Regular walking has been shown to significantly help reduce cancer-related fatigue and increase your energy levels.
*   **Staying Active:** Combining good nutrition with regular physical activity helps you manage your health and stay strong.
*   **Activity Goals:** Physical activity is a key partner to nutrition in helping the body handle the stress of treatment.

## Key Facts
*   **1 in 3** men and women will be diagnosed with cancer in their lifetime.
*   There are currently more than **18 million** cancer survivors alive in the United States today.
*   The American Cancer Society has invested over **$5.6 billion** in research since 1946 to improve patient support.
*   **Nutritional care is a human right**, as established by the Vienna Declaration.
*   Dietary fiber is an "unsung nutrient" that most people are not getting enough of in their daily diet.
