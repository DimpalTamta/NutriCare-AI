# Healthy Dinner Guidelines for Cancer Patients

## Introduction
*   Oncology registered dietitians provide scientifically-supported care to help optimize your health during cancer treatment.
*   A specialized nutrition plan helps you make informed choices about your food to support your individual needs during this crucial time.

## Importance of Dinner
*   Eating enough in the evening helps you maintain a healthy weight, which is the primary nutritional goal during treatment.
*   Proper nutrition provides the energy needed to heal properly and cope with the physical challenges of treatment like chemotherapy or radiation.
*   Attention to evening nutrition can assist in an easier recovery and help rebuild tissues.

## Light and Balanced Dinner
*   Focus on high-calorie and high-protein foods to minimize weight changes, especially if you are feeling weak.
*   Include a wide variety of nutrient-dense foods to ensure you get enough vitamins and minerals.
*   Light exercise during the day can help improve your appetite and digestion for your evening meal.
*   Try eating smaller, more frequent portions if a large dinner feels overwhelming.

## Protein Choices
*   Protein is essential for rebuilding tissues that may be harmed by cancer treatments.
*   **Animal-Based Sources:** Lean meats, poultry, fish, eggs, and dairy products are excellent choices.
*   **Plant-Based Sources:** Beans and lentils are high-quality options that can help improve nutrient deficiencies.

## Easy-to-Digest Foods
*   Choose moist foods, sauces, or gravies to make eating easier if you have dry mouth or swallowing problems.
*   Soups and stews are often well-tolerated and can be packed with nutrients and calories.
*   Bland foods are helpful if you are experiencing nausea or have a sensitive stomach.

## Foods to Avoid Before Bed
*   Limit very greasy or fried foods, as these can worsen feelings of nausea.
*   Be cautious with foods that cause gas and bloating to ensure a comfortable night's rest.
*   Avoid alcohol, as reducing or avoiding consumption is a key factor in lowering cancer risk.

## Hydration Tips
*   Fluids are essential for your body’s basic functioning and to help manage treatment side effects.
*   Use safe drinking water that is free from contaminants like arsenic.
*   If you find it difficult to drink large amounts, try taking small, frequent sips throughout the evening.

## Key Facts
*   Cancer was a leading cause of death globally in 2024, accounting for nearly 10 million lives lost.
*   Approximately 38% of cancers are preventable through healthy lifestyle choices, including a healthy diet.
*   Nutritional care is recognized as a human right for all patients to support their dignity and health.
*   Low fruit and vegetable intake is a major modifiable risk factor for several common types of cancer.
