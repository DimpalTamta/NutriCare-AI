# Meal Recommendation Rules for Cancer Nutrition Chatbot

## Purpose of Meal Recommendation
*   The primary goal of recommending specific meals is to help you maintain a healthy weight throughout your treatment.
*   Proper nutrition provides the energy needed to cope with the daily challenges of procedures like chemotherapy or radiation.
*   The right food choices help rebuild body tissues that may be harmed by cancer treatments.
*   Preventing malnutrition is essential to assist in an easier and faster recovery.

## How Meals Should Be Selected
*   Recommendations are based on customized and scientifically supported nutrition care.
*   Selection logic prioritizes high-calorie and high-protein options if you are feeling weak or are underweight.
*   The system uses a wide variety of foods to ensure you get essential vitamins, minerals, and fluids.

## Ingredient Availability
*   **Information not in sources:** The chatbot should prioritize recipes with ingredients commonly found in local grocery stores to ensure you can easily follow the plan.
*   **Information not in sources:** Simple, few-ingredient recipes should be ranked higher for days when you have low energy.

## Nutritional Balance
*   Priority 1: High-calorie ingredients to minimize weight changes.
*   Priority 2: High-protein sources to support tissue repair.
*   Priority 3: Fluids and nutrient-dense fruits and vegetables to support basic body functioning.

## Symptom-Based Selection
*   **Decreased Appetite:** Recommend small, frequent "mini-meals" instead of large plates.
*   **Nausea/Vomiting:** Prioritize bland foods and clear liquids while avoiding greasy or heavy items.
*   **Taste Changes:** Suggest experimenting with different spices or using plastic utensils if you have a metallic taste.
*   **Mouth Sores:** Filter for soft, moist, and non-acidic foods that won't sting.
*   **Constipation/Diarrhea:** Adjust fiber recommendations and fluid intake based on digestive status.

## Treatment-Based Selection
*   **Pre-Surgery:** Focus on building up strength and nutrient stores.
*   **Post-Surgery:** Recommend easy-to-digest foods that promote wound healing and tissue repair.
*   **Chemotherapy/Radiation:** Select foods that are safe for a potentially weakened immune system.

## Texture-Based Selection
*   **Swallowing Problems (Dysphagia):** Filter for soft, pureed, or liquid-based meals.
*   **Dry Mouth (Xerostomia):** Recommend moist foods with extra gravies, sauces, or broths.

## Hydration Consideration
*   Fluids are flagged as essential for the functioning of every system in your body.
*   The system recommends high-nutrient liquids like broths or smoothies if you are struggling with solid food.

## Personal Preferences
*   **Information not in sources:** The chatbot should learn your likes and dislikes to suggest meals you are more likely to eat when your appetite is low.
*   **Information not in sources:** Users can tag specific favorite "comfort foods" to be suggested during difficult treatment days.

## Cuisine Preferences
*   **Information not in sources:** The system should offer diverse cuisine types (e.g., Mediterranean, Asian, American) based on your cultural background and what sounds appealing.

## Dietary Restrictions
*   The algorithm must respect medical dietary needs, such as managing nutrient deficiencies identified by your care team.
*   **Information not in sources:** Filters must be applied for allergies (e.g., gluten-free, dairy-free) or other health conditions like diabetes.

## When Not to Recommend Certain Foods
*   **Contaminants:** Avoid recommending foods with high risks of aflatoxin (a biological carcinogen found in some crops).
*   **Alcohol:** Avoid or significantly reduce alcohol recommendations as it is a known risk factor for many cancers.
*   **Unsafe Water:** Do not suggest using water from untreated sources that may contain arsenic.

## Recipe Ranking Rules
*   **Rank Higher:** High-protein, high-calorie meals for those with unplanned weight loss.
*   **Rank Higher:** Meals that address your current specific side effects (e.g., bland for nausea).
*   **Rank Higher:** Nutrient-dense options that include a variety of fruits and vegetables.

## Safety Notes
*   **Immunocompromised Status:** Follow strict food safety guidelines if your immune system is weak from treatment.
*   **Medical Oversight:** Always review these recommendations with your doctor or oncology registered dietitian.
*   **Referrals:** If you have serious concerns about weight loss or eating, ask your team for a nutrition referral.
