# Meal Timing Tips for Cancer Patients

## Introduction
*   Oncology registered dietitians provide scientifically-supported nutrition care to help you optimize your health before, during, and after treatment.
*   Proper nutrition is a vital part of your care, and your team is dedicated to helping you make informed choices.
*   Nutritional care is recognized as a human right for all patients to support their dignity and health.

## Why Meal Timing Matters
*   Cancer treatments like chemotherapy, radiation, and surgery can cause a loss of appetite and energy.
*   The primary goal of nutrition during treatment is to maintain a healthy weight so you stay strong.
*   Proper timing helps ensure your body gets the calories and protein needed to heal tissues and cope with new challenges.

## Small Frequent Meals
*   If you are struggling with a decreased appetite, try eating smaller portions more often throughout the day.
*   Instead of trying to eat three large meals, aim for several "mini-meals" or snacks.
*   This approach can help you get enough nutrients without feeling overwhelmed by a full plate of food.

## Eating During Treatment
*   Focus on eating whenever you feel able, regardless of the time of day.
*   Prioritize high-calorie and high-protein foods to help minimize weight changes and maintain your energy.
*   Exercise can sometimes help improve your appetite and digestion issues related to treatment.

## Eating Before Treatment
*   It is very important to maintain proper nutrition before your treatment begins to help your body handle physical stress.
*   Entering treatment well-nourished can lead to an easier recovery.
*   Oncology dietitians can help you create a plan to optimize your health before starting therapy or surgery.

## Eating After Treatment
*   Eating well after treatment or surgery is essential for rebuilding tissues that may have been harmed.
*   Continued attention to nutrition helps you regain your strength and maintain a healthy weight.
*   Your care team provides guidance on nutrition choices for recovery, cancer prevention, and survivorship.

## Managing Appetite Throughout the Day
*   Take advantage of times when you feel well by eating your most nutrient-dense foods then.
*   **Information not in sources:** For many people, appetite is strongest in the morning, making breakfast a good time for a larger meal.
*   Keep easy-to-eat, high-protein snacks nearby so you can eat whenever you have the urge.

## Hydration Timing
*   Fluids are essential for your body to function correctly during and after treatment.
*   **Information not in sources:** If you feel full quickly, try drinking most of your fluids between meals rather than during them.
*   Taking small, frequent sips throughout the day is often easier than drinking large amounts at once, especially if you feel nauseated.

## Key Facts
*   Cancer was a leading cause of death globally in 2024, accounting for nearly 10 million deaths.
*   Approximately 38% of cancers are currently preventable through healthy lifestyle choices, including a healthy diet.
*   Lung, breast, colorectal, and prostate cancers are among the most common types diagnosed worldwide.
*   Many cancers have a high chance of being cured if they are detected early and treated according to best practices.
