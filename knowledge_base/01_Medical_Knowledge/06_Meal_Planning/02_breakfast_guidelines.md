# Healthy Breakfast Guidelines for Cancer Patients

## Introduction
*   Oncology registered dietitians provide comprehensive and scientifically supported nutrition care to help you optimize your health.
*   Proper nutrition is a vital part of your journey before, during, and after cancer treatment.
*   Your dietitian can help you make informed choices that are tailored to your specific diagnosis and needs.

## Why Breakfast is Important
*   **Highest Appetite:** Many individuals undergoing treatment find that their appetite is strongest early in the day.
*   **Energy for the Day:** Starting with a balanced meal provides the necessary energy to cope with the physical challenges of treatment.
*   **Weight Maintenance:** Eating a good breakfast helps you meet your daily goals for calories and protein, which is the primary goal during treatment.

## Characteristics of a Healthy Breakfast
*   **High-Protein:** Essential for rebuilding body tissues that may be harmed by cancer treatments like chemotherapy or radiation.
*   **High-Calorie:** Necessary to help you maintain a healthy weight and prevent unplanned weight loss.
*   **Nutrient-Dense:** Includes essential vitamins and minerals to support your body's basic functioning.
*   **Hydrating:** Incorporates fluids, which are essential for your body to work correctly.

## Recommended Food Groups
*   **Protein:** Eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, lean breakfast meats, and nut butters.
*   **Whole Grains/Carbohydrates:** Oatmeal, whole-wheat toast, cream of wheat, and pancakes.
*   **Fruits:** Fresh berries, bananas, or melons for essential nutrients.
*   **Healthy Fats:** Avocado, olive oil, and nuts to boost calorie intake.

## High-Protein Breakfast Ideas
*   **Soft Scrambled Eggs:** Can be prepared with added cheese or whole milk to increase calories and protein.
*   **Greek Yogurt Parfaits:** Layers of yogurt with fruit and a sprinkle of nuts or granola.
*   **Nut Butter on Toast:** Whole-grain toast topped with peanut or almond butter.
*   **Cottage Cheese with Fruit:** A simple, high-protein option that is often easy to eat.

## Easy-to-Digest Breakfast Options
*   **Well-Cooked Grains:** Oatmeal or cream of wheat made with milk rather than water for extra protein.
*   **Bland Foods:** Plain toast or bagels can be helpful if you are experiencing nausea.
*   **Fruit Smoothies:** Blended fruit with yogurt or protein powder can be easier to consume if you have a sore throat or swallowing issues.
*   **Poached or Soft-Boiled Eggs:** Provides high-quality protein in a texture that is usually easy to swallow.

## Foods to Limit
*   **High-Gas Foods:** Limit foods like broccoli or heavy beans in the morning if you are struggling with gas and bloating.
*   **Very Greasy Foods:** Fried meats or heavy oils may worsen feelings of nausea.
*   **Highly Processed Sugars:** While calories are important, try to focus on nutrient-dense sources to support overall health.

## Breakfast Tips for Common Symptoms
*   **For Nausea:** Focus on bland, cool, or room-temperature foods.
*   **For Taste Changes:** If foods taste metallic, try using plastic utensils or adding different seasonings and spices.
*   **For Sore Mouth or Throat:** Use gravies, sauces, or choose very moist foods to make swallowing more comfortable.
*   **For Decreased Appetite:** Try eating smaller, more frequent portions throughout the morning rather than one large meal.

## Key Facts
*   Cancer accounted for nearly **10 million deaths** globally in 2024, or roughly one in six deaths.
*   Approximately **38% of cancers are preventable** through healthy lifestyle choices, including a healthy diet.
*   Nutritional care is recognized as a **human right** for all patients to support their dignity and health.
*   The primary nutritional goal during treatment is to **maintain a healthy weight**.
*   Many cancers have a high chance of being cured if they are detected early and treated according to best practices.
