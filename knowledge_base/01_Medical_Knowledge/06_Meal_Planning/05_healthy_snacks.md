# Healthy Snack Ideas for Cancer Patients

## Introduction
*   Oncology registered dietitians provide customized and scientifically-supported care to help you **optimize your health** during treatment.
*   Proper nutrition is a vital part of your journey, and your care team is dedicated to helping you make informed choices.
*   Nutritional care is recognized as a **human right** to support your dignity and health throughout cancer care.

## Why Healthy Snacks Matter
*   Cancer treatments, such as chemotherapy and radiation, can cause you to **lose your appetite and energy**.
*   Snacks help you get the extra calories and protein needed to **minimize weight changes** and maintain strength.
*   Proper attention to nutrition can assist in an **easier recovery** and help your body rebuild tissues.
*   Eating small snacks throughout the day can help prevent **malnutrition** when you don't feel like eating large meals.

## When to Eat Snacks
*   If you have a decreased appetite, try eating **smaller, more frequent portions** instead of three large meals.
*   Eat whenever you feel hungry; many people find their appetite is strongest in the morning.
*   Keep snacks nearby so you can eat whenever you have the energy or the urge.

## High-Protein Snacks
*   Protein is essential for **rebuilding body tissues** that may be harmed by cancer treatments.
*   **Dairy options:** Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, and cheese slices are excellent high-protein choices.
*   **Eggs:** Hard-boiled eggs are a simple and effective protein source.
*   **Plant-based options:** Beans, lentils, and nut butters provide high-quality protein.

## High-Calorie Snacks
*   The primary goal during treatment is to **maintain a healthy weight**.
*   **Nuts and seeds:** These are energy-dense and help you get more calories in small amounts.
*   **Healthy fats:** Avocados and snacks prepared with olive oil can boost your calorie intake.
*   **Full-fat dairy:** Choose whole-milk yogurt or rich cheeses if you are losing weight.

## Easy-to-Carry Snacks
*   Keep portable snacks like **nut packs or cheese sticks** with you for appointments or travel.
*   **Information not in sources:** Whole fruits like bananas or oranges are convenient and provide essential vitamins.
*   **Information not in sources:** Whole-grain crackers with nut butter are easy to pack and provide both energy and protein.

## Hydrating Snacks
*   Fluids are **essential for your body's functioning**, especially during treatment.
*   **Smoothies:** Blending fruit with yogurt or milk provides both hydration and calories.
*   **Soups:** Broth-based or creamy soups can help you stay hydrated while providing nutrients.
*   Always use **safe drinking water** and avoid sources that may be contaminated with arsenic.

## Foods to Limit
*   **Alcohol:** You should avoid or significantly reduce alcohol consumption to lower cancer risks.
*   **Tobacco:** Do not use tobacco products, as they are a leading cause of cancer death.
*   **Contaminated foods:** Avoid foods that may contain contaminants like **aflatoxin**.
*   **Information not in sources:** Limit very sugary or "empty calorie" snacks that do not provide the protein or vitamins your body needs to heal.

## Key Facts
*   Cancer accounted for nearly **10 million deaths** globally in 2024, or roughly one in six deaths.
*   Approximately **38% of cancers are preventable** through healthy lifestyle choices, including a healthy diet.
*   The most common cancers worldwide are **lung, breast, and colorectal** cancers.
*   Many cancers have a high chance of being cured if they are **detected early** and treated properly.
*   Low fruit and vegetable intake is a major risk factor for cancer deaths globally.
