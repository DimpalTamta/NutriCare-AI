# High-Calorie Meals for Cancer Patients

## Introduction
*   Oncology registered dietitians provide comprehensive and scientifically supported nutrition care for patients with a cancer diagnosis.
*   They specialize in helping patients optimize their health before, during, and after treatment.
*   Specialized nutritional care is recognized as a fundamental human right to support patient health and dignity.

## Who Needs High-Calorie Meals?
*   Patients undergoing treatments like chemotherapy, radiation, or surgery who are experiencing a loss of appetite and energy.
*   Individuals at an increased risk for malnutrition due to their cancer diagnosis.
*   Anyone experiencing unplanned or undesirable weight loss during their care.
*   Patients who feel physically weak or are considered underweight.

## Benefits
*   **Weight Maintenance:** The primary goal of a high-calorie diet is to help you maintain a healthy weight.
*   **Healing:** Providing enough calories and protein helps your body rebuild tissues that treatment may harm.
*   **Energy:** Proper calorie intake provides the energy needed to cope with the daily challenges of treatment.
*   **Recovery:** Attention to nutrition can lead to an easier and faster recovery process.

## Healthy High-Calorie Ingredients
*   **Animal Proteins:** Lean meats, poultry, and fish.
*   **Dairy and Eggs:** Eggs, milk, cheese, and yogurt.
*   **Plant Proteins:** High-protein beans and lentils.
*   **Information not in sources:** Healthy fats such as olive oil, avocados, nuts, and seeds.

## Meal Building Tips
*   Eat a wide variety of high-calorie and high-protein foods to ensure you get all necessary nutrients.
*   Try to eat even when you do not feel well or do not have a strong desire to eat.
*   Incorporate nutrient-dense foods to ensure your body gets enough vitamins and minerals.
*   Include exercise in your routine, as it can help improve your appetite and digestion.
*   Obtain a referral from your doctor to speak 1:1 with an oncology registered dietitian for a customized plan.

## Healthy Calorie Boosters
*   Choose full-fat dairy products instead of low-fat or skim versions to increase calorie density.
*   Add high-protein ingredients like eggs or beans to soups and casseroles.
*   **Information not in sources:** Mix healthy oils like olive oil or flaxseed oil into grains and vegetables.
*   **Information not in sources:** Use nut butters as a spread or blend them into smoothies and oatmeal.
*   **Information not in sources:** Incorporate liquid nutritional supplements if you find it difficult to eat enough solid food.

## Foods to Avoid
*   **Alcohol:** You should avoid or significantly reduce alcohol consumption, as it is a major risk factor for many cancers.
*   **Contaminated Food:** Be cautious of food contaminants like aflatoxin, which are biological carcinogens.
*   **Tobacco:** Do not use tobacco, as it is responsible for nearly a quarter of all cancer deaths.
*   **Information not in sources:** Avoid "empty calorie" foods that are high in sugar but lack the protein and vitamins needed for healing.

## Sample Meal Ideas
*   **Information not in sources:** Fruit and yogurt smoothies boosted with nut butter or protein powder.
*   **Information not in sources:** Hearty bean and lentil stews made with lean meats and topped with cheese.
*   **Information not in sources:** Soft scrambled eggs prepared with whole milk and cheese.
*   **Information not in sources:** Whole-grain pasta tossed with olive oil, lean chicken, and vegetables.

## Key Facts
*   Cancer was a leading cause of death globally in 2024, accounting for nearly 10 million lives lost.
*   Approximately 38% of cancers are preventable through healthy lifestyle choices, including a healthy diet.
*   Lung, breast, and colorectal cancers are among the most common types diagnosed worldwide.
*   Modifiable risk factors like unhealthy diets and lack of physical activity contribute significantly to the global cancer burden.
*   Early detection and appropriate care give many common cancers a high chance of being cured.
