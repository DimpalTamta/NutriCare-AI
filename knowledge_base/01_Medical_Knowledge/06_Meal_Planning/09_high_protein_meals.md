# High-Protein Meals for Cancer Patients

## Introduction
*   **Nutrition is a vital part** of your journey before, during, and after cancer treatment.
*   Oncology registered dietitians provide customized, scientifically-supported care to help you **optimize your health**.
*   Specialized high-protein diets are often recommended to help you stay strong and manage the physical demands of treatment.

## Who Needs High-Protein Meals?
*   Patients undergoing treatments such as **chemotherapy, radiation, immunotherapy, or surgery**.
*   Those who are at an **increased risk for malnutrition** due to their diagnosis.
*   Individuals experiencing **unplanned weight loss** or those who feel physically weak.
*   Patients who need help rebuilding body tissues that may be harmed during treatment.

## Benefits
*   **Weight Maintenance:** The primary nutritional goal during treatment is to **maintain a healthy weight**.
*   **Tissue Repair:** Protein provides the building blocks to repair and rebuild cells damaged by cancer therapies.
*   **Energy and Strength:** Proper protein intake helps you maintain the energy needed to cope with treatment challenges.
*   **Easier Recovery:** Paying close attention to protein can assist in an **easier recovery process**.

## High-Protein Ingredients
*   **Animal-Based:** Focus on lean meats, poultry, fish, eggs, and dairy products.
*   **Plant-Based:** Incorporate plenty of beans and lentils.
*   **Information not in my sources:** Other excellent sources include nuts, seeds, soy products (like tofu), and high-protein grains like quinoa.
*   **Information not in my sources:** High-protein oral supplements or powders may be recommended if you cannot meet your needs through food alone.

## Combining Protein Sources
*   Add high-protein ingredients like **eggs or beans** to existing dishes like soups and casseroles.
*   **Information not in my sources:** Mix plant and animal proteins, such as adding chickpeas to a chicken salad, to increase total protein in one meal.
*   **Information not in my sources:** Use milk instead of water when preparing oatmeal or soups to boost the protein content easily.

## Meal Suggestions
*   **Information not in my sources:** Soft scrambled eggs with added cheese or beans.
*   **Information not in my sources:** Greek yogurt parfaits topped with nuts and seeds.
*   **Information not in my sources:** Chicken or tofu stir-fry served with whole grains.
*   **Information not in my sources:** Cottage cheese paired with fruit or whole-wheat crackers.

## Protein Distribution Throughout the Day
*   Aim to include a source of **protein at every meal and snack** to ensure a steady supply for your body.
*   If you have a **decreased appetite**, try eating smaller, high-protein "mini-meals" more frequently throughout the day.
*   **Eat when your appetite is strongest**, which for many people is early in the morning.
*   Keep high-protein snacks nearby so you can eat whenever you feel able.

## Key Facts
*   Cancer was a leading cause of death globally in 2024, accounting for nearly **10 million lives lost**.
*   Approximately **38% of cancers are preventable** through healthy lifestyle choices, including a healthy diet.
*   Nutritional care is recognized as a **human right** for all patients to support their dignity and health.
*   Lung, breast, and colorectal cancers are among the most common types diagnosed worldwide.
