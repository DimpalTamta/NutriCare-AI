### Zinc: Wound Healing and Immune Support

## Introduction
*   Zinc is an essential mineral that plays a vital role in many of your body's daily functions. 
*   For cancer patients, maintaining adequate mineral levels like zinc is a key part of specialized nutrition care provided by oncology registered dietitians.
*   Proper nutrition helps you optimize your health before, during, and after treatment.

## Why Zinc is Important
*   **Support Body Systems:** It is essential for the basic functioning of your body while you are healing.
*   **Information not in sources:** Zinc is a major component of your immune system, helping your body defend itself against infections.
*   **Information not in sources:** It is necessary for your sense of taste and smell, which are often affected by cancer treatments.

## Functions
*   **Tissue Repair:** Zinc supports the body in rebuilding and repairing tissues that may be harmed by chemotherapy, radiation, or surgery.
*   **Recovery Support:** Proper attention to nutrients like zinc can assist in an easier recovery and help you maintain the energy to cope with treatment.
*   **Information not in sources:** It is involved in cell growth and the creation of DNA.

## Food Sources
*   **Animal Protein Sources:**
    *   Lean meats and poultry.
    *   Fish and seafood.
    *   Eggs and dairy products.
*   **Plant-Based Sources:**
    *   Beans and lentils.
    *   **Information not in sources:** Nuts, seeds, and whole grains.
    *   **Information not in sources:** Zinc-fortified breakfast cereals.

## Deficiency
*   **Malnutrition Risk:** An inability to maintain proper nutrient levels puts you at an increased risk for malnutrition during treatment.
*   **Slow Healing:** A lack of essential minerals can make it harder for your body to repair itself.
*   **Information not in sources:** Signs of deficiency can include a loss of appetite, slow wound healing, and a decreased sense of taste.
*   **Information not in sources:** Hair loss and diarrhea can also be signs that your zinc levels are low.

## Daily Requirements
*   General guidance for cancer patients is to eat a **wide variety of high-calorie and high-protein foods** to support weight maintenance.
*   Your specific needs may increase if you are recovering from surgery or experiencing side effects like diarrhea.
*   Always consult your oncology registered dietitian for a personalized plan based on your medical history.

## Key Facts
*   Cancer was a leading cause of death globally in 2024, accounting for nearly **10 million deaths**.
*   Nutritional care is recognized as a **human right** for every patient to support their dignity and health.
*   Approximately **38% of cancers** can be prevented through healthy lifestyle factors, including a healthy diet.
*   The primary goal of nutrition during treatment is to **maintain a healthy weight**.
*   Early detection and proper nutrition can significantly improve the probability of survival and quality of life.
