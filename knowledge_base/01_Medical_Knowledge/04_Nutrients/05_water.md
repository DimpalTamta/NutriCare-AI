### Water: The Foundation of Hydration During Cancer Treatment

## Introduction
*   Water and other fluids are absolutely essential for your body to function properly during cancer treatment.
*   Staying hydrated helps you optimize your health before, during, and after your treatment journey.
*   Oncology registered dietitians are available to help you make informed choices about your fluid needs.

## Why Water is Important
*   **Body Function:** Fluids are critical for every basic system in your body to work correctly.
*   **Energy Maintenance:** Staying hydrated helps you maintain the energy needed to cope with the challenges of treatment.
*   **Recovery:** Proper attention to hydration and nutrition can lead to an easier recovery.
*   **Side Effect Management:** Fluids are necessary to replace what is lost during treatment-related side effects like vomiting or diarrhea.

## Daily Fluid Requirements
*   General guidance is to try to consume enough fluids to support your body's daily needs and maintain a healthy weight.
*   Your specific fluid needs may increase depending on the type of treatment you receive, such as chemotherapy or surgery.
*   Consult your healthcare team or an oncology dietitian for a personalized fluid plan tailored to your medical status.

## Signs of Dehydration
*   **Loss of Energy:** Feeling significantly more tired or having a sudden drop in energy levels.
*   **Malnutrition Risk:** An inability to maintain fluid intake puts you at an increased risk for malnutrition.
*   **Digestion Issues:** Difficulty with digestion or a change in appetite can sometimes be linked to poor hydration.

## Best Sources of Fluids
*   Plain water is always an excellent choice for hydration.
*   A wide variety of other fluids can be used to meet your daily goals.
*   If you are losing weight, your dietitian may recommend high-calorie and high-protein liquid supplements to provide both fluids and energy.

## Tips to Stay Hydrated
*   Try to keep a bottle of water or another favorite drink with you throughout the day for easy access.
*   If side effects like nausea or taste changes make drinking difficult, ask your doctor for a nutrition referral to speak with a specialist.
*   Monitor your weight regularly to ensure you are staying hydrated and maintaining your weight.

## Key Facts
*   Cancer is a leading cause of death globally, accounting for nearly 10 million deaths in 2024.
*   Nutritional care, including proper hydration, is recognized as a human right for all patients.
*   Approximately 38% of cancers can currently be prevented through healthy lifestyle choices, including a healthy diet.
*   The main goal of nutrition during treatment is to maintain a healthy weight to help you stay strong.
*   Many cancers have a high chance of being cured if they are detected early and treated according to best practices.
