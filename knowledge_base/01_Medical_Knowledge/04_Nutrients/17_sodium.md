### Sodium: Essential Electrolyte for Fluid Regulation

## Introduction
*   Sodium is an essential mineral and electrolyte that helps your body function properly.
*   It is one of the many nutrients that oncology registered dietitians monitor to help improve **nutrient deficiencies**.
*   Maintaining the right balance of sodium is a key part of specialized nutrition care during your cancer journey.

## Why Sodium is Important
*   **Fluid Balance:** Sodium is essential for maintaining the right amount of fluid in your body, which is critical for every system to work correctly.
*   **Energy and Strength:** Proper mineral levels help you maintain the energy needed to cope with the challenges of treatment.
*   **Recovery Support:** Paying attention to nutrients like sodium can assist in an **easier recovery** from procedures like surgery.
*   **Information not in sources:** It is also vital for helping your muscles contract and your nerves send signals throughout your body.

## Functions
*   **Supporting Body Systems:** Sodium helps keep your body’s basic functioning stable while you are healing.
*   **Regulating Hydration:** It works with other electrolytes to ensure your body stays hydrated, especially if you are losing fluids due to side effects.
*   **Information not in sources:** Sodium plays a major role in controlling your blood pressure and blood volume.

## Food Sources
*   **Natural Sources (Information not in sources):** Found in small amounts in eggs, milk, meat, and some vegetables.
*   **High-Calorie/High-Protein Options:** Broths and soups are often used in high-calorie diets to provide both energy and sodium.
*   **Information not in sources:** **Added Sodium:** Most sodium in a standard diet comes from table salt, processed meats, canned foods, and fast food.

## Excess Sodium Intake
*   **Information not in sources:** Eating too much sodium can cause your body to hold onto extra water, leading to swelling (edema) in your legs or feet.
*   **Information not in sources:** High sodium intake can also lead to increased blood pressure.
*   If you notice a **rapid weight gain** (several pounds in a few days), notify your healthcare team immediately, as this may be fluid retention.

## Daily Requirements
*   General guidance for cancer patients is to eat a **wide variety** of foods to maintain a healthy weight.
*   Your specific needs for sodium may change if you are experiencing side effects like severe vomiting or diarrhea.
*   Ask your doctor for a **nutrition referral** to speak with a registered dietitian who can create a personalized plan for you.

## Key Facts
*   Cancer was a leading cause of death globally in 2024, accounting for nearly **10 million deaths**.
*   Nutritional care, including the management of minerals, is recognized as a **human right** for all patients.
*   Approximately **38% of cancers** can be prevented through healthy lifestyle choices, which include a balanced diet.
*   Low fruit and vegetable intake is a major modifiable risk factor for several types of cancer.
*   The primary goal of nutrition during active treatment is to **maintain a healthy weight** to help you stay strong.
